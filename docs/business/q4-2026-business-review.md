# LearnLoop: Q4 2026 Business Review

**Author:** Head of Product
**Date:** 3 November 2026
**Audience:** Leadership team and external advisors

## 1. Where we are

LearnLoop is an online course platform that people pay for monthly. One plan, 19 USD per month, gives access to the whole catalog: 12 self-paced courses in four areas (Data, Cloud, Leadership, Design), each made of six to ten short video lessons with quizzes.

We started September with about **420 paying learners**, about 7,980 USD in monthly recurring revenue. We did not run any acquisition activity this autumn, and new subscriptions over the period were close to zero. So the business today depends on the learners we already have.

The team that runs the service day to day (learner support, account questions, course operations) is **three people**. Product and engineering are a separate, small group.

Comparing the two most recent 30-day periods (4 Sep to 3 Oct, then 4 Oct to 2 Nov):

- **Learners actively taking lessons:** 340 → 195 (down about 43%). The fall happened over roughly two weeks from mid-September, then levelled off; it has not recovered.
- **Lessons completed:** 3,616 → 2,262 (down 37%).
- **Courses finished** (a learner completing every lesson of a course): 457 → 298 (down 35%).
- **Cancellations:** 16 in September and 34 in October. Weekly cancellations went from about 4 to 5 a week in September to 9 and then 13 in the last two weeks.
- **Active subscriptions** on 2 November: 366, about 6,950 USD in monthly recurring revenue (down 13% from September).

Price, plan and course content stayed the same during this period. Service health was stable: we had no outages, error rates stayed very low and page speed did not change. Our regular releases went out every two weeks or so, as usual.

## 3. What worries us

1. **Fewer learners are progressing.** About half of our paying learners now open a lesson in a given week (193 of 366). At the start of September it was close to four in five (326 of 420).
2. **Cancellations are accelerating.** If the current weekly rate continues, we lose about 50 subscribers a month, roughly 950 USD of monthly revenue each month, with nothing coming in to replace them.
3. **We do not yet understand why.** The decline is not explained by anything we changed on purpose.
4. **Our capacity is limited.** Three people already handle support and operations. Anything that adds manual work for them will not be sustainable.

## 4. Priorities for Q4 2026

In order of importance:

1. **Keep our subscribers.** Bring weekly cancellations back to the September level (about 4 a week) or lower by the end of the quarter.
2. **More learners actively progressing every week.** Get back to at least 300 learners completing or starting lessons each week. That is where we were in September.
3. **Higher course completion.** More learners should finish the courses they start. We want the number of courses finished per month to be back above 450.

**Guardrail:** support workload must not grow. The three-person team should not handle more tickets, messages or manual tasks per week than it does today. We will judge any change against this as well as against the three goals above.

## 5. What we are asking for

We want an independent view of what is driving these numbers and which outcomes are realistic for this quarter. We are not committing to any particular approach yet. Please look at the evidence first.
